About PrairieComps

PrairieComps is a free website showing fair-market asking prices for used farm and heavy equipment across Alberta and the Canadian Prairies. It’s built for farmers, contractors, and dealers who want comps by make, model, year, and hours — without a paywall.

How we get prices

We collect publicly posted dealer inventory pages only when robots.txt and published terms allow it. Every listing in our dataset keeps a source URL and the date we saw it. We also cite publicly published market aggregates (with attribution) where useful.

See docs/SOURCES.md in the project for the full allow/deny table.

What PrairieComps is not

  • Not an appraisal, valuation opinion, or lending guide
  • Not auction hammer prices (unless a future permitted auction source is added)
  • Not affiliated with John Deere, CLAAS, Case IH, Caterpillar, Ritchie Bros, or any OEM

Business model

Buyers use the site free. Later, dealers can pay $99–$199 CAD/mo for Featured / Premium placement and claimed dealer pages. B2B data products are on the roadmap.
